# Empyrean fan game — two-path research

Research date: 2026-10-03. Two build paths, one goal: a free, public, fan-made game that earns reach and followers.

## Target

The operator, as a fan creator. Not for distribution to CeminiSuite.

## Summary

The fandom is peaking right now. A free, transformative, clearly-labelled fan project is viable on either path. A real build takes 8-14 weeks, so it cannot ride this wave — but a small, clippable map can. Nobody has made the Empyrean Minecraft mod yet.

## The triggering moment

- **Dragonkind** (dragonkind.com/play) is an official interactive "Threshing" experience by Yarros Ink, Inc. Story by Rebecca Yarros, art by Andreas Rocha and Arancia Studio.
- **Launched 2026-10-01**, the in-universe Threshing date, two days after the *Threshing Day* collection (2026-09-29).
- Yarros states **half a million people entered on day one**. The site queued and crashed. r/fourthwing opened three megathreads.
- It is a branching story, not a personality quiz. Email signup, enter the Rider's Quadrant, cross into Threshing Valley, a dragon chooses you, you get a shareable card. About 10 minutes, replayable, with death and retry.
- It runs a **countdown to May 2027** for "manifesting signets" — a built-in next spike.
- TikTok now shows about **2.2M posts** for "what dragon claims you," **1.3M** for "what dragon am I," **513K** for the signet quiz.

## Path A — Roblox

- **Tech.** Obbies and gauntlets are easy. Checkpoints and teleports are stock. Custom riders use `HumanoidDescription`. The hard part is the **rideable flying dragon**: a `VehicleSeat` is quick but janky, a CFrame controller is smooth but heavy code.
- **Discovery.** Over 90% of traffic starts on Home. The 2026-06-15 rework ranks on play-through rate, first-play bounce, play days per user, and playtime per user, all as per-user averages. There is **no cold-start boost** for new experiences. Ads cost about $20 per 1,000 plays, and advice is "no less than $100 a campaign." Organic reach with zero following is near zero.
- **Proof case.** **Wings of Fire — 100M+ visits**, openly a fan project for a dragon book series.
- **Effort.** 8-14 person-weeks for a slice, 6-12 months for a full experience.
- **Blunt.** "Roblox will distribute for you" is a myth. BookTok is the distributor; Roblox is the host.

## Path B — Minecraft Bedrock

- **Tech.** Custom entities and rideable flying dragons are proven (Dragon Mounts, Age of Berk). Custom dimensions are **experimental and void-only** — no terrain generation — so use an Overworld map plus teleports. Scripting is `@minecraft/server` 2.x.
- **Tooling.** Blockbench (the standard), bridge., Snowstorm, and the in-game **Bedrock Editor** for the build.
- **Discovery.** MCPEDL (~7.8M visits a month) favours recency and early download velocity. CurseForge and Planet Minecraft add long-tail search. Aggregators scrape and rehost MCPEDL files, which gives free reach but no analytics. Marketplace is paid-only.
- **Precedent.** **No Empyrean Bedrock add-on or map exists.** Dragon-mount add-ons are a proven, high-demand genre. The niche is open.
- **Effort.** 8-14 person-weeks for a slice, 4-7 months for a fuller release.
- **Version note.** Bedrock moved to year versioning. Current stable is **26.20**.

## Legal

- **No Empyrean fan takedown exists** that this research could find. Absence of evidence, not safety.
- **The rights holder is monetising.** An official WizKids card game (*Fourth Wing: Formations*, 2027); an Entangled + Chernin Group partnership (Dec 2025) aimed at "gaming"; Dragonkind itself; and an Amazon MGM adaptation.
- **"Free and non-commercial" does not immunise a derivative work.** Fair use is a defence, not permission.
- **What survives:** transformative work, original art, no book text, no cover art, no series name in the title, no monetisation, clear "fan-made" labelling.
- **Takedown asymmetry.** A rights holder can remove a project in days. A takedown is itself a press event, but it caps the project.
- **The durable lesson:** fan projects that became careers did it by pivoting to original IP (Sonic Mania, Them's Fightin' Herds, The Forgotten City).

## The product gap

Dragonkind is a 10-minute one-shot: bond a dragon, get a card, leave. The gap is an **ongoing** world — a persistent Rider's Quadrant with a gauntlet, Threshing flights, and a wing that grows. Copying the book does not travel; filling a gap does.

## Recommendation

Two moves.

1. **Now.** Ship a small Bedrock map with one clippable moment (the Parapet) and post dev clips into the live wave.
2. **Next spike.** Build the persistent game for May 2027 or Conscription Day (July 15).
