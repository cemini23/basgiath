# Distribution and content plan

The paste-ready download text, six clip scripts, and the tag list are in `docs/LISTING.md`.

## Where it ships (free only)

| Channel | What goes there | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| MCPEDL | `.mcworld` + `.mcaddon` | About 7.8M visits a month. Favours recent uploads and early download velocity. |
| CurseForge (Bedrock) | Same files | Steady long-tail search. |
| Planet Minecraft | Same files | Community base, more Java-weighted. |
| Reddit r/fourthwing, r/Minecraft | A clip plus a link | Check each subreddit's self-promo rules first. |
| TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts | The clip | This is the real distributor. |

Do **not** use the Minecraft Marketplace. It is paid-only, so it is off-limits for this project.

## The rule that decides the outcome

The map will not be found by itself. **The clip is the distribution.** Roblox and MCPEDL both give zero traffic to a new upload. The traffic comes from the video you post, and the download is the conversion.

## Timing

There are two windows, not one.

- **Now (Oct 2026).** The Dragonkind wave is peaking. Ship the small map and the clips into this window.
- **Next spike.** Dragonkind runs a countdown to **May 2027**. Conscription Day is **July 15**, every year. Aim the real game — the persistent world — at one of those.

## The honest expectation

Most fan uploads get almost no traction. The ones that work either had a paid push or a pre-built audience. Your only free lever is the clip, posted consistently. Treat the map as the excuse to post, not as the product that sells itself.

## The long game

Every fan project that became a career did it by pivoting to original work. The fan map is the audition. Keep the audience, change the name.
