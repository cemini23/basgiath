# IP rules — read before posting anything

This project is fan work. These rules keep it alive.

1. **Never sell it.** No paid access, no Marketplace, no ads, no paywalled builds. The map is free, and it stays free. Optional tips support the creator's own build time. They never buy access, and no money changes hands for the fan content itself.
2. **No official art or assets.** Do not use the book covers, the Dragonkind art, the official map illustrations, or any logo. Build everything from vanilla blocks.
3. **Series and character names stay out of the ids and the strings.** The place name is allowed in the title. Do not put the book series name, character names, or dragon names into file ids, entity ids, or pack strings.
4. **No book text.** Do not paste dialogue or passages. Write original lines.
5. **Label it clearly.** Every download page and every video says: "Fan-made. Not official. Not affiliated with Rebecca Yarros, Entangled Publishing, or Red Tower Books."
6. **Transform, do not copy.** Build an evocative rendition from the descriptions. Do not trace or copy the official artwork, and do not attempt a one-to-one replica of any licensed illustration.

## Why this matters here

The rights holder is moving into games. There is an official card game coming, a capital partnership aimed at gaming, and an Amazon adaptation. A fan project that looks like a competing product is a bigger target than one that looks like a tribute. Stay a tribute.

A close copy of the official art is the fastest route to a takedown. A block-built evocation is not.

## Precedent

- Free, transformative, clearly-labelled fan projects survive. **Wings of Fire Roblox** passed 100 million visits under the book series' own name.
- Projects that monetised or copied assets got removed. Pokémon Brick Bronze, Pokémon Uranium, and AM2R were all taken down.
- Minecraft's own rules allow free mods. You must show the "not official" disclaimer. Never use "Minecraft" as the primary title.
