# Canon reference

The map is an evocation of the *Empyrean* series by Rebecca Yarros. This file records the canon facts that the build must respect. It is a working reference. It is not a copy of the books.

**Read this with `docs/IP-RULES.md`.** That file is the law. This file is the source material.

## How to use this file

1. Read the beat you are about to build.
2. Build the shape and the order from the "Canon" lines.
3. Follow the "Build note" lines for the Minecraft translation.
4. Never copy the book text or the official art. See the art inventory and the naming policy below.

## Confidence

Most numbers below come from fan wikis and reading guides, not from the books themselves. They agree with each other in most places. The set is marked `NEEDS CHECK` where the sources disagree or where only one source states the number.

This file was checked by two independent free models on 2026-10-04. Both confirmed the facts as written. They raised two points. See `briefs/2026-10-04_canon-verification.md` for the full log.

1. **The Presentation Day date is not in the books.** The text ties Presentation to the Gauntlet, and fixes Threshing to October 1. The "September 29" date is a fan-wiki reconstruction. One model said September 30. Treat the exact date as uncertain. The order is not in doubt.
2. **The Riders Quadrant keep line is a direct quote from the book.** It is also Article One, Section One of the Dragon Rider's Codex. `docs/IP-RULES.md` rule 4 bans book text. Do not carve the exact line into the map. Write an original line for the arch.

# The first year, in order

The first year has seven beats. The order matters.

| # | Beat | When |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | Parapet | Conscription Day, mid-July (about July 15) |
| 2 | Formation | The same day |
| 3 | College | About two months, July to September |
| 4 | Gauntlet | Timed run on Presentation Day |
| 5 | Presentation | Two days before Threshing (fan date: September 29) |
| 6 | Threshing | October 1, always |
| 7 | Signet | Weeks to months after the bond |

**Two corrections to the common plan:**

- The Gauntlet run and Presentation are the **same day**. Practice starts about two and a half weeks before.
- Threshing is **October 1, every year**, whatever the weather. This is stated in the text. Presentation is two days before.
- The exact Presentation date is a fan reconstruction, not a line in the books. Build the **order** and the **gap**, not a calendar date.

## 1. Parapet — Conscription Day, mid-July

The crossing that starts everything. This is the clip for the project.

### Canon

- A narrow stone bridge, 200 feet above the Iakobos River.
- 18 inches wide (45 cm). No rails.
- The bridge is highest at the midpoint. Painted lines mark each quarter and the halfway point.
- Entry: the southern turret of the main college. A ground-level door. A 250-step spiral staircase. The turret is dark and narrow, with windows along the curve.
- Exit: the last 10 feet have low walls on both sides. Then a 12-foot drop into the courtyard.
- About 15% of the candidates die on this day.
- Codex rule: a cadet may keep only the items they carried across the Parapet. This rule matters later, in the Gauntlet.

### Build note

- The current span is 32 blocks up. Canon is about 61 blocks. **NEEDS CHECK** on the exact height in blocks.
- One block wide is a fair stand-in for 18 inches. One block reads wider than canon, but it is playable.
- The current build has a two-block gap in the span. Canon has no gap. Keep the gap only if the clip needs a jump. Mark it as a deliberate game device.
- Canon has the span highest at the midpoint. A gentle arch matches the book better than a flat deck.

## 2. Formation — the same day

### Canon

- In the courtyard of the Riders Quadrant, right after the crossing.
- The courtyard is shaped like an angular teardrop. The rounded end is a giant outer wall, at least 10 feet thick. Stone halls run along the sides. A four-story building is carved into the mountain.
- The courtyard holds about a thousand riders.
- The courtyard walls are 10 feet thick and 8 feet tall. There is **one** opening. That opening is the Parapet.
- The units: 4 wings. Each wing has 3 sections (Flame, Claw, Tail). Each section has 3 squads. Each squad holds 15 to 20 cadets.
- First-year cadets stand in the back two rows of the squad square.
- Roll call includes the death roll. Scribes read the names of the cadets who died.
- Ranks: Wingleader and Section Leader are third years. An exceptional second year can lead a squad.

### Build note

- The current build has a flat "Quad" platform. Canon is an enclosed courtyard, not an open plaza.
- Add the wall ring: 10 blocks thick, 8 blocks tall, one opening toward the Parapet.
- Add a roll-call beat. It is the cheapest way to teach the wing, section, and squad structure.

## 3. College — about two months

### Canon

- Basgiath sits on Basgiath Mountain, in Morraine Province, on the Iakobos River. It overlooks the river and the Vale.
- The lower level holds four quadrants: Scribe, Healer, Infantry, and Administration.
- The upper level is the Citadel. The Citadel belongs to the Riders Quadrant. It holds barracks, classrooms, command offices, and the flight field.
- The Citadel is an L-shaped group of tall stone buildings, carved into the mountain. They are built to withstand fire.
- The Dragon Rotunda links the academic wing and the dorms. It is also the entrance to the gathering hall, the commons, and the library.
- The Rotunda is three stories tall. It has polished gray marble floors and a domed glass ceiling. Four doors open from the courtyard: two to the academic wing, between orange and black pillars, and two to the dorms.
- The keep has one giant arched door. The arch carries a carved line about a rider and their dragon. The exact line is **book text** and is also Codex law. Do **not** carve it into the map. Write an original line. See the confidence note at the top.
- The dorms are a three-story building. The first floor holds at least 156 cadets in four rows of beds. A bonded rider gets a private room.
- Classes: sparring, Battle Brief, history, wielding, and flight maneuvers.
- Flight leathers are handed out only after Threshing. Too many cadets die before it.

### Build note

- The build has no college building at all. This is the biggest gap. The Citadel is the heart of the map.
- Suggested first structures: the courtyard wall ring, the rotunda shell with four doors, the keep with its arched door, and the dorm block.
- The build has three spruce barracks. Canon calls for one large dorm block, plus classrooms.

## 4. Gauntlet — timed run on September 29

### Canon

- A vertical obstacle course, cut into the ridgeline between the Citadel and the flight field.
- The cliff guards the Vale. That is why it is called the Gauntlet.
- Five switchbacks. Each one turns 180 degrees. Each one is harder than the last.
- The obstacles, in order:
  1. A spinning log, 15 feet long, parallel to the cliff face.
  2. Raised granite pillars of increasing height.
  3. A giant rotating wheel with one opening.
  4. Giant stone hands that the cadet jumps on or hugs.
  5. A near-vertical "chimney".
  6. A final near-vertical wooden ramp.
- Safety ropes run every 6 feet, top to bottom. Using a rope adds 30 seconds to the time.
- Each squad gets 9 one-hour practice sessions.
- A wingleader records the times at the top.
- In the book, 171 first-years attempt it. Two die on the course. The other 169 finish. By Threshing the group is down to 147.
- A steep staircase beside the switchbacks leads to the flight field. Cadets use it **after** Presentation, not during the Gauntlet.

### Build note

- The Gauntlet is absent from the current build. Add the cliff and the five switchbacks.
- In Minecraft, the "obstacles" become parkour: a rotating log, rising pillars, a wheel with one hole, hanging blocks, and a climbable chimney.
- The safety ropes can be vines or chains. Add a time penalty if the player touches one.

## 5. Presentation — September 29, after the Gauntlet

### Canon

- On the flight field. The field sits in a natural box canyon at the top of the Gauntlet ridgeline.
- The field lies between the Citadel and the Vale. The Vale's entrance is at the narrowest part of the field, by a seasonal waterfall.
- Squads walk a footpath through the meadow, in order of their Gauntlet times. Faster squads walk first.
- The dragons form up a few feet back and watch. A cadet must not approach a dragon.
- A dragon can burn a cadet it dislikes. In the book, six cadets die during the walk.
- Presentation lets the dragons look. It is **not** the choosing.

### Build note

- The flight field is absent. Add the box canyon, the footpath, and the dragon line.
- The walk is a strong second clip. A short, tense corridor is enough.

## 6. Threshing — October 1

The bond. The dragon chooses. The cadet does not.

### Canon

- October 1, always, whatever the weather or the weekday.
- In the Vale: a bowl-shaped forested valley, beneath and southwest of the Citadel. Squat trees, wildflower slopes, greenery running south.
- Cadets spread out alone. There is no ceremony and no matching.
- A cadet follows their feelings. A dragon that already chose a cadet at Presentation will call to them.
- Second- and third-year riders watch. They cannot interfere. First-years can act on old vendettas.
- A dragon needs the Right of Benefaction from its den head before it can attend.
- After the bond:
  1. The dragon marks the rider with a relic. The relic usually takes the shape of the dragon.
  2. Flight trials. The dragon flies and turns. The rider must hold their seat. If the rider falls, the dragon either catches them or lets them die.
  3. The rider lands and tells the roll-keeper the dragon's full name. Only the rider and the roll-keeper know that name.
- The works crew add a 6-foot dais and bleachers, with a medical station at the end.
- Book numbers: the novel says 101 dragons attend. The wiki says 100, down from 137 and 163 in the two years before. Sources disagree on the exact count. Use "about 100".
- The novel says 147 cadets reach Threshing. Many bond nothing.
- An unbonded first-year may repeat the year.

### Build note

- Threshing is absent as an event. This is the beat Grok proposed to build first. It is the right first choice.
- The Vale must stop reading as a summoning pad. Make it a forested dell with open ground.
- The dragon must walk to the player, or choose the player. The player must not pick the dragon. This is the core rule of the scene.

## 7. Signet — weeks to months after the bond

### Canon

- The bond first gives lesser magic: move objects, lock doors, power a pen, shield the mind, switch mage lights.
- The signet appears within about six months of the first channel. If it does not appear, the power kills the rider.
- The signet reflects the rider, not the dragon. A stronger dragon and a stronger bond make a stronger signet.
- A rare second signet can pass down a bloodline. This is a "legacy signet".
- Signets need focus. Strong emotion makes them misfire.

### Build note

- The current build gives the power at the plaza stone, on day one. Canon puts it weeks after the bond. Move it.
- The stone can still open the form. Move the form to the post-bond state, in the valley, after the dragon chooses the player.
- The archetypes are the map's own. The rules ban character names, book text and official art; they do not limit which powers the map may have, so the set can grow. See `DESIGN.md` and the naming policy below.

# Naming policy

This updates `docs/IP-RULES.md` rule 3. Confirm the change before the build relies on it.

| Category | Allowed? | Rule |
|----------|----------|------|
| Place names | Yes | Parapet, Basgiath, the Vale, the Gauntlet, the Citadel, the flight field, Navarre, the Iakobos River, Aretia, Morraine |
| Dragon names | Yes, with a lookalike | A favorite dragon may reuse its book name only if the model matches the canon color and tail type. Keep the name out of file ids, entity ids, and pack strings |
| People names | No | Violet, Xaden, Dain, Mira, Lilith, Brennan, Rhiannon, Imogen, Garrick, Bodhi, Sloane, Ridoc, Sawyer, Liam, Jack. Do not use these |
| Series and book titles | Title only | Empyrean, Fourth Wing, Iron Flame, Onyx Storm, Threshing Day. Never in file ids, entity ids, or pack strings |
| Official art and logos | Never | No covers, no map art, no Dragonkind art |
| Book text | Never | Write original lines only |

**Precedent.** `docs/IP-RULES.md` cites *Wings of Fire Roblox*. That game uses its series' own name and passed 100 million visits. Note two limits: it is a different series and a different publisher, and it shows the series name only, not character names. A search for a Roblox game named "Wings of Flame" found no match. **NEEDS CHECK** if you have a link.

**Risk ranking**, highest first: official art > character names > dragon names > series name > place names. The place names are the safest. The dragon names are next. Character names are the line the user drew.

# What is not yet verified

Checked by two free models on 2026-10-04. Still open:

- The exact length of the Parapet. No source states it. Only the width (18 inches) and the height (200 feet) are given.
- The exact Presentation Day calendar date. See the confidence note.
- The exact count of dragons. The novel says 101. The wiki says 100.
- The exact height of the Parapet in Minecraft blocks. The book gives 200 feet, about 61 blocks.
- The Conscription Day date. The official game launched on July 15 to mark it. Treat mid-July as safe.
- The Roblox precedent. No game named "Wings of Flame" was found. `docs/IP-RULES.md` already cites "Wings of Fire Roblox", a different series.

The full model outputs are in `briefs/2026-10-04_canon-verification.md`.
