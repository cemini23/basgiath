### Root cause (if debugging)
The far pass was introduced precisely because pass 1 cannot place blocks in unloaded chunks on a phone. The author then reasoned that air fills are "already placed" by pass 1 and dropped them from the far set to fit the 80,000 air budget. That reasoning is inverted: the far set exists *because* pass 1 fails for those chunks, so far air is the only place those air fills can ever run on a phone. The budget gate then rewarded the deletion, and the static bench (which only replays stage files) could not see the loss. The fix is to keep the far air and bring the total under 80,000 by trimming volume, not by skipping the retry.

### Confidence
high — the `is_far`/`LOADED` logic and the prompt's own list of dropped far air make the failure mechanical. Would change my mind only if the far air were provably inside the loaded box (it is not: boxes reach x=123, z=140) or if a third pass re-placed it (none exists).

### Unique angle
The static bench is the trap: `bench_static.py` globs only `stage_*.mcfunction`, so it will keep reporting "static bench ok" and the Parapet contract green even after the far chasm air is gone — the one test that could catch this is blind to the far files. A reviewer who trusts the green bench will miss that the phone path is now broken.
