### Root cause (if debugging)
The primary root cause is a coordinate-space mismatch: `build.mcfunction` teleports the player to Y=80 and summons the `build_anchor` there, but `build_map.py` generates all geometry with absolute Y values relative to Y=0 (ground at Y=-2, deck at Y=8, valley at Y=-6). Every stage function therefore places blocks 80 blocks above their intended positions. The `live.mcfunction` wind and rescue selectors inherit the same offset, so the wind zone sits above the deck and the chasm rescue misses the top of the chasm. The secondary root cause is CI fragility: `ci.yml` pins non-existent action versions (`@v7`) and a non-existent Pillow version (`12.2.0`), so the green CI claim cannot be reproduced on a fresh runner.

### Confidence
high — the coordinate mismatch is arithmetic and visible in the inlined files; the CI version pins are verifiable against public release history. What would change my mind: a Bedrock client run showing the map builds correctly at Y=80, which would mean the anchor is intentionally at Y=80 and the geometry is meant to be relative to that, contradicting the `ground()` and `chasm()` absolute coordinates.

### Unique angle
Other models may focus on the dragon model or the signet form. The sharpest defect is that the entire map is built 80 blocks too high because the anchor is summoned at Y=80 while the geometry is authored at Y=0. This breaks the Parapet, the chasm rescue, the wind push, and the dragon summon in one stroke, and it is invisible to `test_release.py` because that test only checks command syntax and fill volumes, not world-space placement.
